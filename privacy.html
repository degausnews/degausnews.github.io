Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

News Pipeline (“the app”) is a private tool run by its owner on their own computer to publish videos to YouTube channels that the owner controls.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload): used only to upload videos and thumbnails to the channel the owner selected.
  • Read-only channel information (youtube.readonly): used only to show the connected channel's name and avatar so the owner can choose where each video goes.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a private file readable by the owner's user account. They are never sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or for training AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by disconnecting the channel in the app, which deletes the stored token.
