News Pipeline

News Pipeline is a private, self-hosted tool that turns news articles into narrated video digests and uploads them to YouTube channels owned by its operator.

It runs only on its owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and there are no user accounts or sign-ups.

When a YouTube channel is connected, the tool asks Google for permission to upload videos to that channel and to read the channel's name, so that the right channel can be chosen for each video.
