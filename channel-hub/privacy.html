Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Channel Hub (“the app”) is a private tool run by its owner on their own computer to view statistics of YouTube channels that the owner controls.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube account, read-only (youtube.readonly): channel name, avatar, subscriber count, list of videos and their public view counts and scheduled publish times.
  • YouTube Analytics, read-only (yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly): daily views, watch time, subscriber changes and estimated revenue of the owner's channels.

The app only reads data. It does not upload, change or delete anything on YouTube.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in the operating system's secure keychain. Collected statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or for training AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by disconnecting the channel in the app, which deletes the stored token and the collected statistics.
